# Research model (working specification)

The journal follows the course's three analytical pillars:

1. Currency fundamentals: central-bank stance, inflation, employment, growth, activity, communications.
2. External factors: geopolitics, commodities, trade exposure, risk sentiment, cross-market relationships.
3. Positioning and pricing: COT/CFTC, implied rates, yield differentials, CESI, options and other market internals.

A research entry should progressively distinguish:

- **Fact** — what was published or observed, with source and timestamp.
- **Interpretation** — what the fact may imply economically or for policy.
- **Market pricing** — what is already reflected in rates/prices/positioning.
- **Scenario** — what could happen and over what horizon.
- **Counterargument** — what contradicts the scenario.
- **Invalidation** — what observation would force a revision.

Neutral, insufficient, and conflicting evidence are valid outcomes. No synthetic score should be used to force a directional bias.
